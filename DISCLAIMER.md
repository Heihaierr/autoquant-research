# Disclaimer

This repository contains research methodology and documented failures. It is not
investment advice.

Nothing here recommends any security, strategy, or allocation. The case material
describes what happened in one research program on one set of instruments during
one historical period. Past results — including the failures — do not predict
future outcomes.

The templates are illustrative code, not a production trading system. They have
no order routing, no risk limits enforced at runtime, and no exchange
connectivity. Using them to trade real capital without independent verification
would be a mistake of exactly the kind this repository documents.

No live strategy parameters are published here, and none should be inferred from
the examples.
